How it works

Place two bets. One wins. You keep the gap.

Two sportsbooks priced the same bet differently, so you take one side at each. If the Over hits you make $42. If the Under hits you make $44. You don't need to follow the team, or even the sport. Lock in profit by betting on both sides before the game starts, no promos needed.

  • You place it. You keep the difference. Your money stays in your own accounts. Every bet lands in your bet tracker automatically.
